# GitHub Trending – 2026-08-09 (Sunday)

## Editor's Take
Two repos deserve immediate attention this week. **andrewyng/openworker** is the most significant agent release of the month: Andrew Ng shipping a privacy-first, locally-run AI coworker (25+ integrations, any LLM provider, approval gates before consequential actions) is a direct statement about where agentic AI is headed — and its design philosophy around human oversight is rich territory for content. On the weekly side, **firecrawl/anydoc** exploding to ~13K stars in under a week confirms what anyone building LLM pipelines already knows: clean, reliable document-to-Markdown conversion is genuinely hard, and Firecrawl solving it in Rust with Python/Node bindings lands in a real gap. Both repos are highly contentable for an AI agent and LLM tooling audience.
