# GitHub Trending – 2026-09-05 (Saturday)

## Editor's Take
Two repos this week genuinely deserve your attention. First, **anthropics/commerce-agents** is a fresh reference blueprint from Anthropic's own org showing exactly how to wire Claude into production agentic workflows — retail, telecom, entertainment — with real examples, not toy demos; it's the clearest public signal yet of how Anthropic expects agents to be structured for business use cases. Second, **2akouwu/reverify** is tackling one of the hardest practical problems in AI agent design — hallucination — with a principled "propose, then verify" architecture backed by an MCP server; the pattern is domain-agnostic and the reverse-engineering proving ground makes it unusually rigorous. If you cover either, cover both: they sit at opposite ends of the stack (blueprint vs. infrastructure) and together tell a complete story about where production AI agents are heading this week.
