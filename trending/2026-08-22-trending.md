# GitHub Trending – 2026-08-22 (Saturday)

## Editor's Take
Two repos this week are worth a closer look. **yetone/cumora** (2,900 stars in ~5 days) is a cross-platform team-chat app where AI agents are first-class teammates with bring-your-own brains — Claude Code and Codex are explicitly supported — making it one of the cleaner implementations of the "agents as coworkers" thesis that's been circulating as theory. **CopilotKit/OpenBot** (2,314 stars) goes a step further by giving each AI coworker its own isolated computer (browser, filesystem, tools), with every action pre-declared and post-logged; the `agent-governance` topic signals that enterprise concerns about auditability are moving from talking point to product feature. Both dropped this week and are still climbing.
