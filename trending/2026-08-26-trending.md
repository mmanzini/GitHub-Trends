## Editor's Take
Two repos are genuinely worth a closer look this week. **deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness** is the big story of the month: DeepSeek shipped a plugin-based agent harness that has already pulled nearly 200k stars in under two weeks — a signal that the ecosystem is converging on plugin/extension architectures for AI runtimes, and the pace of adoption is extraordinary content fuel. Both sit at the intersection of agents, Claude/MCP tooling, and dev experience.
