# GitHub Trending – 2026-08-06 (Thursday)

## Editor's Take
Two repos stand out this week. **trycompai/crm** is the first open-source CRM built "agentic-first" — agents are first-class users of the system, not bolt-on integrations — and it's a concrete, codebase-level example of what an AI-native product architecture actually looks like; great content for showing your audience the difference between "has AI features" and "designed for agents." **firecrawl/anydoc** from the Firecrawl team is equally worth your attention: it's a Rust-native document-to-Markdown converter (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, EPUB, etc.) with Node.js and Python bindings, and it fills a real gap in LLM data pipelines where clean, structured text ingestion is still painful. Both dropped in the last 7 days and together illustrate two sides of the same agentic stack: the infrastructure to feed agents clean data, and the product layer built around them.
