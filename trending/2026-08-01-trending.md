## Editor's Take
The standout story this week is **Kimi K3**: Moonshot AI open-sourced their 2.78-trillion-parameter model (MoonshotAI/Kimi-K3, 7.8k stars in days), and the community immediately rallied to make it runnable—`sqliteai/waste` lets you stream its weights directly from NVMe to get past the RAM ceiling, and `gavamedia/deltafin` wraps the whole thing in an OpenAI-compatible API server for local agents. That trifecta—open frontier model + two community inference rigs—is a content angle worth seizing. Also notable: **yc-software/qm** (4.9k stars), a multiplayer agent harness for collaborative work, is a rare early-stage project in the "multiple agents, one task" space that's generating genuine developer excitement.
