# GitHub Trending – 2026-08-12 (Wednesday)

## Editor's Take
Two repos this week deserve immediate attention. **watermarks-remover** is the week's most provocative project: a Claude agent skill that strips AI provenance marks from documents and images — C2PA metadata, SynthID statistical patterns, Unicode zero-width watermarks — and it's racked up 2,100+ stars in under 24 hours. It sits directly at the center of the content-authenticity debate and is literally tagged `claude` and `agent-skill`, making it prime content material. The other standout is **h3.c**, where Redis creator Salvatore Sanfilippo (antirez) built a lean C inference engine for MiniMax H3 on Mac — a signal that LLM inference is maturing into a systems programming problem, not just a Python/PyTorch one.
